# Customer support procedures

These procedures are operating templates, not published policy. Final response promises must match the approved store policies and fulfillment provider.

## Before responding

Verify the requester using the order number plus the order email. Do not send full addresses, payment details, internal notes, API keys, or Stripe identifiers by email.

## Address change

1. Check whether production or fulfillment has started.
2. If it has not started, update the provider destination and record the change on the order.
3. If it has started, explain that rerouting is not guaranteed and contact the carrier/provider.
4. Never change the country or shipping charge without reviewing tax and postage impact.

## Cancellation

1. Confirm the order status and whether production has started.
2. Cancel only when the approved policy permits it.
3. For a paid order, use the admin refund action and verify the Stripe refund ID/status.
4. Record the reason and do not fulfill a cancelled/refunded order.

## Damaged, defective, or incorrect print

1. Request the order number and photos of the print, packaging, label, and damage/defect.
2. Compare the claim with the product and fulfillment record.
3. Offer the approved replacement or refund remedy.
4. Track repeat defects by product, size, paper, provider, and packaging type.

## Lost or delayed package

1. Verify the tracking number and carrier’s latest scan.
2. Apply the carrier/provider waiting period before declaring a parcel lost.
3. Confirm the destination with the customer without repeating the full address in an unsecured message.
4. Open the carrier/provider claim and apply the approved replacement/refund remedy.

## Refund

1. Verify eligibility and amount against the Stripe payment and order total.
2. Use the admin refund action; never request card details.
3. Confirm the order becomes refunded or partially refunded and fulfillment is stopped where possible.
4. Tell the customer that bank posting time is controlled by their payment provider.

## Escalate immediately

Escalate disputes, fraud signals, threats, legal/privacy requests, repeated fulfillment failures, data exposure, or any request that cannot be reconciled with Stripe and Neon records.
